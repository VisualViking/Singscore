How to test

Use headphones if possible, stand in a quiet room, tap Start microphone, then sing one comfortable sustained note for 2–3 seconds. The blue dot shows the pitch your voice is producing; the green target is the nearest equal-tempered note.

This version measures pitch, not lyrics or song recognition. Results can be affected by room noise, microphone quality, and vocal harmonics.
